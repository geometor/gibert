K001 — Neuberg Cubic
====================

.. enunciation::

   Isogonal pivotal cubic with pole X(6) and pivot X(30) (Euler infinity point): locus of points M such that M, its isogonal conjugate M*, and X(30) are collinear, equivalently MM* is parallel to the Euler line.

.. figure:: /_static/svg/K001.svg
   :alt: K001 — Neuberg Cubic
   :align: center

   **K001** (*Neuberg Cubic*, pK(X6, X30)) on scalene reference triangle :math:`ABC` with circumcircle guide and verified Kimberling centers.
