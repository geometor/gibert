Types / item

Content of type 'item'

K001 — Neuberg Cubic

Isogonal pivotal cubic with pole X(6) and pivot X(30) (Euler infinity point): locus of points M such that M, its isogonal conjugate M*, and X(30) are collinear, equivalently MM* is parallel to the Euler line.

K002 — Thomson Cubic

Isogonal pivotal cubic with pole X(6) and pivot X(2) (centroid): locus of points M such that M, its isogonal conjugate M*, and X(2) are collinear.

K003 — McCay Cubic

Isogonal pivotal cubic with pole X(6) and pivot X(3) (circumcenter): locus of points M such that M, its isogonal conjugate M*, and X(3) are collinear; its three real asymptotes concur at X(2) making pairwise angles of 60 degrees.

K004 — Darboux Cubic

Isogonal pivotal cubic with pole X(6) and pivot X(20) (de Longchamps point): central at X(3) and locus of points M whose pedal triangle is a cevian triangle.

K005 — Napoleon–Feuerbach Cubic

Isogonal pivotal cubic with pole X(6) and pivot X(5) (nine-point center): passes through the Napoleon points X(17), X(18) and Kosnita point X(54).

K006 — Orthocubic

Isogonal pivotal cubic with pole X(6) and pivot X(4) (orthocenter): locus of points M such that M, its isogonal conjugate M*, and X(4) are collinear.

K007 — Lucas Cubic

Isotomic pivotal cubic with pole X(2) and pivot X(69) (isotomic conjugate of X(4)): locus of points M whose cevian triangle is orthologic to ABC.

K008 — Droussent Cubic

Circular isotomic pivotal cubic with pole X(2) and pivot X(316) (isotomic conjugate of X(67)).

K009 — Lemoine Cubic

Nodal cubic with perpendicular nodal tangents at X(3): locus of points M whose cevians MA, MB, MC meet the perpendicular bisectors of BC, CA, AB in three collinear points.

K010 — Simson Cubic

Locus of the tripoles (trilinear poles) of the Simson lines of the reference triangle ABC, with an acnode at the centroid X(2).

K011 — Tucker Cubic

Original Tucker cubic: locus of points M whose cevian triangle has the same signed area as the orthic triangle of ABC.
